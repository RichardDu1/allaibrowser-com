## Best AI Scraping Tools (2026)

AI scraping tools represent the next generation of web data extraction. Instead of writing fragile selectors, you describe what you want and the AI figures out how to get it.

### 1. AgentQL — Best for Simple Extraction

AgentQL's natural language query interface makes it the most accessible AI scraping tool. Describe what data you want in plain English, and AgentQL handles the rest. It's ideal for quick, one-off extraction tasks.

### 2. Browserbase — Best for Scale

Browserbase provides the infrastructure backbone for large-scale scraping operations. Its managed browsers, built-in stealth, and proxy rotation make it the best choice for production scraping at scale.

### 3. Skyvern — Best for Complex UIs

Skyvern's vision-based approach makes it uniquely capable of handling websites with complex JavaScript rendering or dynamic content that other tools struggle with. It's the best choice for the hardest scraping problems.
