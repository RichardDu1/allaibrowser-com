## Overview

Browserbase provides cloud-hosted, managed headless browsers with built-in stealth, proxy rotation, and session recording. It's designed for teams that need reliable browser infrastructure at scale without managing their own infrastructure.

## Key Features

- **Cloud Browser Farm**: Thousands of browsers on demand
- **Stealth Mode**: Built-in anti-detection
- **Proxy Rotation**: Automatic IP rotation
- **Session Recording**: Record and replay browser sessions
- **Stagehand Integration**: Closely integrated with the Stagehand framework

## Why It Matters

Browserbase solves the infrastructure problem of running headless browsers at scale. Combined with Stagehand, it provides a complete cloud-native browser automation solution for teams that don't want to manage their own browser infrastructure.
