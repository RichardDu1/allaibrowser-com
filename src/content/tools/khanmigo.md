Khanmigo is an AI tutor that helps teachers create custom lesson plans by analyzing student performance data. It integrates with Khan Academy's extensive content library, offering personalized practice sets and video recommendations. The tool is particularly effective for math and science subjects. Pricing starts free for basic use, with a $44/month premium plan for unlimited access.
