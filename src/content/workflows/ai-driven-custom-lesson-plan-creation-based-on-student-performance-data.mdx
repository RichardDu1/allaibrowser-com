## Step 1: Collect Student Performance Data

Export student performance data from your school's LMS (e.g., Google Classroom, Canvas) or gradebook. Focus on metrics like quiz scores, assignment completion, and concept mastery. Save as CSV or Excel.

## Step 2: Upload Data to AI Lesson Plan Tool

Use **MagicSchool.ai** (free tier available) to upload your data. Navigate to the "Lesson Plan Generator" and select "Custom from Data". Paste or upload your CSV. The AI will analyze patterns and identify class-wide and individual gaps.

## Step 3: Generate Differentiated Lesson Plans

In MagicSchool, choose the subject and grade level. The AI will produce a lesson plan with:
- **Learning objectives** tied to standards
- **Differentiated activities** for below, at, and above grade level
- **Assessment ideas** aligned to gaps

Review and edit the plan. Use the "Export" button to save as Google Doc or PDF.

## Step 4: Personalize with Khanmigo

For deeper personalization, use **Khanmigo** (Khan Academy's AI tutor). Input individual student performance data (e.g., "Student A scored 60% on fractions"). Khanmigo generates a custom practice set and suggests video resources.

## Step 5: Integrate with Eduaide.Ai

Use **Eduaide.Ai** to create supplementary materials like worksheets, quizzes, and slide decks. Select "Generate from Data" and upload your performance summary. The AI will create materials targeting weak areas.

## Step 6: Implement and Track

Deliver the lesson plan in class. After the lesson, collect new performance data (e.g., exit tickets) and repeat the cycle. Use the AI tools to track progress over time.

## Pro Tips
- **Privacy**: Anonymize student names before uploading (use IDs).
- **Iterate**: Run the workflow weekly for continuous improvement.
- **Combine tools**: Use MagicSchool for main plan, Khanmigo for individual tutoring, and Eduaide for materials.
