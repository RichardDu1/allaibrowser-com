## AI-Powered Academic Paper Abstract Generation and Citation Formatting for Researchers

This workflow guides researchers through generating high-quality abstracts and formatting citations using AI tools integrated into your browser.

### Step 1: Install the Paperpal Browser Extension
- Go to the Chrome Web Store and search for "Paperpal".
- Click "Add to Chrome" to install the extension.
- Pin the extension to your toolbar for easy access.

### Step 2: Generate an Abstract with Paperpal
- Open the research paper or manuscript you want to abstract.
- Click the Paperpal icon in your browser toolbar.
- Select "Generate Abstract" from the menu.
- Paperpal will analyze the text and produce a concise, academic abstract.
- Review and edit the abstract as needed.

### Step 3: Install MyBib Citation Generator
- Visit the MyBib website (mybib.com) and install the browser extension.
- The extension adds a "Cite" button to your browser.

### Step 4: Capture Citations from Web Sources
- Navigate to a scholarly article or webpage you want to cite.
- Click the MyBib "Cite" button.
- The extension automatically extracts metadata and generates a citation in your preferred style (APA, MLA, Chicago).
- Copy the citation to your clipboard or export to your reference manager.

### Step 5: Manage Your Citations
- Use MyBib's online dashboard to organize citations into projects.
- Export your bibliography in various formats (BibTeX, Word, etc.).

### Step 6: Combine Abstract and Citations
- Paste the generated abstract into your paper.
- Insert the formatted citations using your reference manager.

### Tips for Best Results
- Always proofread AI-generated abstracts for accuracy and tone.
- Double-check citations against original sources for correctness.
- Use Paperpal's additional features like grammar check and plagiarism detection for a polished manuscript.
