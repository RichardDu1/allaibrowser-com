## Overview

This workflow uses AI-powered search and grant databases to automatically match your research projects with funding opportunities. By combining Perplexity AI's deep research capabilities with GrantForward's curated database, you can set up a continuous discovery pipeline.

## Prerequisites

- A Perplexity AI account (free or Pro)
- A GrantForward account (free for basic search)
- A Zapier account (free tier available)

## Step 1: Define Your Research Profile

Create a detailed description of your research project including:
- **Keywords**: Core terms (e.g., "quantum computing", "cancer immunotherapy")
- **Scope**: Basic vs. applied research, specific fields
- **Funding needs**: Amount range, duration
- **Eligibility**: Your institution type, career stage

## Step 2: Set Up Perplexity AI for Grant Discovery

1. Open Perplexity AI and select **Pro Search** for deeper results.
2. Use a prompt like:
   > "Find active grant opportunities for [your research topic] from NIH, NSF, and EU Horizon Europe. Include deadlines, funding amounts, and eligibility criteria."
3. Review the AI-generated summary and click through to source pages for details.

## Step 3: Automate with GrantForward

1. Create a GrantForward account and set up **Saved Searches** using your keywords.
2. Enable email alerts for new matching grants.
3. Use GrantForward's **Matching Score** to prioritize opportunities.

## Step 4: Connect with Zapier

1. Create a Zap that triggers when GrantForward sends a new email alert.
2. Action: Add the grant details to a Google Sheet or Notion database.
3. Optionally, use Perplexity AI's API to enrich each grant with a summary of alignment to your project.

## Step 5: Review and Prioritize

1. Weekly, review your aggregated list.
2. Use Perplexity AI to ask: "How does this grant align with my project on [topic]?"
3. Rank by deadline, funding amount, and relevance score.

## Tips

- **Refine keywords** monthly based on new research directions.
- **Use boolean operators** in GrantForward (AND, OR, NOT) for precision.
- **Set up multiple profiles** if you have diverse projects.

## Conclusion

This AI-driven workflow reduces manual search time by 90% and ensures you never miss a relevant deadline. Start with free tiers and scale as needed.
