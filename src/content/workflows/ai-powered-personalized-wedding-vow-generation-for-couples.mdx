## AI-Powered Personalized Wedding Vow Generation for Couples

This guide helps you craft unique, heartfelt wedding vows using AI tools. No writing experience needed.

### Step 1: Gather Your Love Story

Before using AI, collect key details:
- How you met
- Favorite memories
- What you admire about your partner
- Inside jokes or shared dreams

Write these down in a document (e.g., Notion).

### Step 2: Choose an AI Tool

We recommend **ChatGPT** (free tier available) for its conversational ability. Alternatively, use **Notion AI** if you already use Notion.

### Step 3: Craft Your Prompt

Use this template in ChatGPT:

```
I need personalized wedding vows. Here is our love story:
- How we met: [details]
- Favorite memory: [details]
- What I admire: [details]
- Inside joke: [details]
- Shared dream: [details]

Please write two versions of vows (one for me, one for my partner) that are heartfelt, about 150 words each, and include a promise section. Use a warm, sincere tone.
```

### Step 4: Review and Customize

Read the AI output. Adjust any phrases to sound more like you. Add personal touches (e.g., a nickname).

### Step 5: Practice Together

Read the vows aloud. Ensure they flow naturally. Make final edits.

### Step 6: Finalize

Copy the final vows into a nice document or vow book. Practice until confident.

### Tips for Best Results
- Be specific in your prompt (more details = better output).
- Use the AI to generate multiple options, then mix and match.
- Keep vows under 2 minutes each.

### Privacy Note
Avoid sharing overly sensitive personal data. Use generic placeholders if needed.
