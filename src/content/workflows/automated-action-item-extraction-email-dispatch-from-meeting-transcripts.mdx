## Overview

This workflow automates the extraction of action items from meeting transcripts and sends them via email to participants. It uses Otter.ai for transcription and AI-powered action item detection, Zapier for automation, and Gmail for email delivery.

## Prerequisites

- Otter.ai account (free or Business plan)
- Zapier account (free tier available)
- Gmail account

## Step 1: Set Up Otter.ai for Action Item Extraction

1. **Create an Otter.ai account** at [otter.ai](https://otter.ai).
2. **Record or upload a meeting transcript** – Otter.ai automatically transcribes meetings and highlights action items using AI.
3. **Review action items** – In the transcript, Otter.ai identifies and tags action items with a checkbox icon. You can manually add or edit them.
4. **Enable Otter.ai Zapier integration** – Go to Otter.ai settings > Integrations > Zapier, and connect your account.

## Step 2: Create a Zapier Workflow

1. **Log in to Zapier** and click **Create Zap**.
2. **Set trigger: Otter.ai – New Action Item**
   - Choose Otter.ai as the app.
   - Select trigger event: **New Action Item**.
   - Connect your Otter.ai account.
   - Test the trigger to ensure it pulls action items.
3. **Set action: Gmail – Send Email**
   - Choose Gmail as the app.
   - Select action event: **Send Email**.
   - Connect your Gmail account.
   - Configure the email:
     - **To**: Use dynamic data from the trigger (e.g., meeting participants' emails). You may need to map participant emails from Otter.ai (if available) or use a static list.
     - **Subject**: `Action Items from [Meeting Title]`
     - **Body**: Compose a template that includes the action item text, assignee (if available), and due date. Use Zapier's formatter to structure the data.
4. **Test and publish** the Zap.

## Step 3: Customize Email Template

In the Gmail action, use a template like:

```
Hi team,

Here are the action items from our meeting:

{{action_item_text}}

Best,
[Your Name]
```

You can also include a link to the full transcript.

## Step 4: Automate and Monitor

- The Zap runs automatically whenever a new action item is detected in Otter.ai.
- Monitor the Zap history in Zapier for any errors.
- Optionally, add a delay or filter to batch multiple action items into one email.

## Tips

- Use Otter.ai's Business plan for team collaboration and custom vocabulary.
- For more advanced extraction, combine with ChatGPT via Zapier to summarize action items.
- Ensure participant emails are captured in Otter.ai (e.g., via calendar integration).

## Conclusion

This workflow eliminates manual note-taking and follow-up, ensuring no action item is missed. It's scalable for teams of any size.
