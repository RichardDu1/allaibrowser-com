## Overview

This workflow automates the creation of case study drafts from recorded customer interviews. By using AI transcription and writing tools, you can quickly generate structured, compelling narratives that highlight customer success.

## Prerequisites

- A recorded customer interview (audio or video file)
- Accounts for Otter.ai, Jasper AI, and Notion (free tiers available)

## Step 1: Transcribe the Interview with Otter.ai

1. Upload your interview recording to Otter.ai.
2. Let Otter.ai generate an automatic transcript with speaker labels.
3. Review the transcript for accuracy and edit any misrecognized terms (e.g., product names).
4. Export the transcript as a text file or copy it to your clipboard.

## Step 2: Extract Key Insights

1. Open the transcript in Otter.ai and use the "Highlight" feature to mark key quotes, pain points, and results.
2. Create a summary of the interview by asking Otter.ai's AI to generate a meeting summary.
3. Copy the highlights and summary into a Notion page for reference.

## Step 3: Draft the Case Study with Jasper AI

1. Log in to Jasper AI and select the "Case Study" template from the document types.
2. Paste the transcript summary and key quotes into the input fields.
3. Customize the tone (professional, conversational) and length (short, medium, long).
4. Generate the first draft. Jasper will produce a structured case study with sections like Challenge, Solution, Results, and Testimonial.
5. Review the draft and edit for brand voice and accuracy.

## Step 4: Refine and Format in Notion

1. Copy the Jasper draft into a Notion page dedicated to the case study.
2. Add visual elements: customer logo, product screenshots, and pull quotes.
3. Use Notion's database to track case study status (Draft, Review, Published).
4. Share the draft with stakeholders for feedback.

## Step 5: Automate Repetitive Steps (Optional)

1. Use Zapier to connect Otter.ai and Jasper AI: when a new transcript is ready, automatically trigger Jasper to generate a draft.
2. Set up a Notion database to store all case studies and automate status updates.

## Best Practices

- Always get customer approval before publishing.
- Keep transcripts organized in a central repository.
- Use consistent naming conventions for files.

## Troubleshooting

- If Jasper's output is too generic, provide more specific quotes and context.
- If Otter.ai misidentifies speakers, manually correct speaker names in the transcript.
