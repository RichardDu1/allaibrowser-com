## Step 1: Identify Key Competitors and Products

List your top 5-10 competitors and the specific products you want to monitor. Focus on best-sellers and high-margin items.

## Step 2: Set Up Price Tracking with PriceNinja

1. Go to [PriceNinja](https://priceninja.com) and create a free account.
2. Add your competitors' product URLs into PriceNinja's dashboard.
3. Configure tracking frequency (e.g., every 6 hours) and select the price fields to monitor.
4. Enable email alerts for price changes.

## Step 3: Create a Centralized Price Database in Google Sheets

1. Open Google Sheets and create a new spreadsheet named "Competitor Price Tracker".
2. Add columns: Product Name, Competitor, Current Price, Last Checked, Alert Triggered.
3. Use the **IMPORTXML** or **IMPORTHTML** functions to pull live prices if needed, but for automation we'll use Zapier.

## Step 4: Automate Alerts with Zapier

1. Log in to [Zapier](https://zapier.com) and create a new Zap.
2. **Trigger**: Choose PriceNinja as the trigger app and select "New Price Change".
3. Connect your PriceNinja account and select the products to monitor.
4. **Action**: Choose Google Sheets and select "Add Row" to log the price change.
5. Add a second action: Choose Email (or Slack) and send a formatted alert with product name, old price, new price, and competitor.
6. Test the Zap and turn it on.

## Step 5: Set Up Threshold Alerts for Critical Drops

1. In PriceNinja, set custom alerts for price drops below a certain percentage (e.g., 10% below your price).
2. Use Zapier's filter step to only trigger alerts when the price change exceeds your threshold.

## Step 6: Monitor and Adjust Pricing Strategy

1. Review the Google Sheet weekly to spot trends.
2. Use the data to adjust your own prices dynamically (e.g., match or undercut competitors).
3. Consider integrating with a repricing tool like Prisync or RepricerExpress for automatic adjustments.

## Step 7: Scale and Refine

1. Add more competitors and products as needed.
2. Experiment with different alert thresholds.
3. Share the dashboard with your team for collaborative decision-making.

## Troubleshooting Tips

- If PriceNinja doesn't detect prices, ensure the URL is correct and the page loads without login.
- For dynamic websites, use PriceNinja's browser extension to manually select price elements.
- Check Zapier logs if alerts are not firing.
