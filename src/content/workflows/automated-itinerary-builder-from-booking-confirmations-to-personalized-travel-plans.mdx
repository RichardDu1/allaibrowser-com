## Overview

This workflow automates the creation of travel itineraries by connecting your email (booking confirmations) and client preference forms to a central itinerary builder. You'll use Zapier to capture data, Google Sheets as a database, OpenAI's GPT-4 to generate narrative descriptions, and Canva to produce a branded PDF.

## Prerequisites

- A Zapier account (free tier works)
- A Google account (Sheets)
- An OpenAI API key (or ChatGPT Plus for manual steps)
- A Canva account (free or Pro)
- Email inbox (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)

## Step 1: Set Up Email Parsing with Zapier

1. **Create a new Zap** in Zapier.
2. **Trigger**: Choose your email app (e.g., Gmail) and select "New Email Matching Search".
3. **Filter**: Set search criteria to match booking confirmation emails (e.g., subject contains "Booking Confirmation" or sender is "reservations@airline.com").
4. **Action**: Add a step to parse the email body. Use Zapier's Formatter to extract key fields: 
   - `Hotel Name`
   - `Check-in Date`
   - `Check-out Date`
   - `Confirmation Number`
   - `Flight Number` (if applicable)
   - `Departure/Arrival Times`
5. **Test**: Send a sample email to ensure extraction works.

## Step 2: Capture Client Preferences

1. **Create a Google Form** with fields: 
   - Client Name
   - Travel Style (Luxury, Budget, Adventure, etc.)
   - Interests (Food, History, Nature, etc.)
   - Dietary Restrictions
   - Preferred Activities
2. **Connect Form to Google Sheets**: Responses will automatically populate a sheet.
3. **Zapier Integration**: Create a second Zap that triggers on new form submission and stores the data in a separate sheet or appends to the main itinerary sheet.

## Step 3: Build a Central Itinerary Database in Google Sheets

1. **Create a new Google Sheet** with columns:
   - `Client Name`
   - `Booking Source` (email or form)
   - `Hotel Name`
   - `Check-in`
   - `Check-out`
   - `Flight Details`
   - `Preferences`
   - `Generated Itinerary` (will be filled later)
2. **Zapier Action**: Set up a Zap that adds rows to this sheet whenever new booking data or preferences arrive.

## Step 4: Generate Itinerary Narrative with GPT-4

1. **Create a Zap** that triggers when a new row is added to the itinerary sheet.
2. **Action**: Use OpenAI (GPT-4) integration in Zapier.
3. **Prompt Template**:
   ```
   You are a travel itinerary writer. Based on the following booking details and client preferences, create a day-by-day itinerary in a friendly, professional tone. Include recommendations for meals, activities, and local tips.

   Booking Details:
   - Hotel: {{Hotel Name}}
   - Check-in: {{Check-in}}
   - Check-out: {{Check-out}}
   - Flight: {{Flight Details}}

   Client Preferences:
   - Travel Style: {{Travel Style}}
   - Interests: {{Interests}}
   - Dietary Restrictions: {{Dietary Restrictions}}

   Output the itinerary in plain text with clear day headings.
   ```
4. **Output**: Store the generated text in the `Generated Itinerary` column of the sheet.

## Step 5: Design a Branded PDF with Canva

1. **Create a Canva template** for itineraries (use a pre-made travel itinerary template).
2. **Set up Canva API** or use Zapier's Canva integration (if available). Alternatively, manually copy-paste.
3. **Automation**: Use a Zap that triggers when the `Generated Itinerary` column is updated, then uses Canva's "Create Design from Text" action to populate the template.
4. **Export as PDF** and save to Google Drive or email to the client.

## Step 6: Deliver the Itinerary

1. **Final Zap**: Send an email to the client with the PDF attached.
2. **Optional**: Add a step to update your CRM (e.g., HubSpot) with the itinerary status.

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Test with sample data** before going live.
- **Use labels/filters** in Gmail to automatically forward booking emails to a specific folder.
- **Regularly review** extracted data for accuracy.
- **Customize the GPT prompt** to match your agency's tone.

## Troubleshooting

- **Email parsing fails**: Use Zapier's built-in email parser or try a dedicated tool like Mailparser.
- **GPT-4 rate limits**: Upgrade to a paid OpenAI plan or use GPT-3.5 for lower volume.
- **Canva integration limits**: Canva's Zapier integration may require a Pro account; alternatively, use a Google Docs template and convert to PDF.
