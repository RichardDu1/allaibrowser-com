## Overview

This workflow enables university professors to automatically generate structured study guides from lecture transcripts and textbook content using AI. By combining transcription tools, note-taking apps, and AI summarization, you can produce high-quality study materials in minutes.

## Prerequisites

- A Notion account (free or paid)
- An Otter.ai account (free tier available, Pro for longer transcripts)
- ChatGPT Plus subscription (for GPT-4 access)
- Lecture transcripts (e.g., from Otter.ai) and textbook PDFs or digital text

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Transcribe Lectures

Use **Otter.ai** to automatically transcribe your lectures. Upload recorded lecture audio or connect Otter to your Zoom/Teams meetings. Otter provides accurate real-time transcription and speaker identification.

- **Tip:** For best results, ensure clear audio and minimal background noise.
- Export transcripts as plain text or markdown.

### Step 2: Extract Textbook Content

If your textbook is in PDF format, use a PDF-to-text converter (e.g., Adobe Acrobat, online tools) to extract the relevant chapters. Alternatively, copy-paste text from digital textbooks.

- **Note:** Respect copyright; only use content you have permission to reproduce.

### Step 3: Combine and Structure in Notion

Create a new Notion page for the study guide. Use the following structure:

- **Course Name**
  - **Module 1**
    - Key Concepts
    - Definitions
    - Important Dates/Formulas
    - Summary
  - **Module 2**
    - ...

Paste the lecture transcript and textbook excerpts into separate sections or as toggle lists for easy reference.

### Step 4: Generate Study Guide with ChatGPT

Use **ChatGPT Plus** (GPT-4) to process the content. Provide the following prompt:

> "You are an expert study guide creator. Based on the following lecture transcript and textbook excerpts, create a structured study guide for students. Include key concepts, definitions, important dates/formulas, and a summary for each module. Use bullet points and clear headings. Content: [paste transcript and textbook text]"

ChatGPT will output a well-organized study guide in markdown format.

### Step 5: Refine and Format in Notion

Copy the ChatGPT output into your Notion page. Adjust formatting, add images or diagrams if needed, and ensure accuracy. Notion supports markdown, so headings, lists, and bold text will be preserved.

- **Pro Tip:** Use Notion's database feature to create a template for future study guides.

### Step 6: Share with Students

Once finalized, share the Notion page with your students via a shareable link. You can also export as PDF for offline access.

## Best Practices

- **Review AI output:** Always verify facts and correct any misinterpretations.
- **Update regularly:** Keep study guides current with each semester.
- **Combine sources:** Use both lecture and textbook content for comprehensive coverage.

## Troubleshooting

- **Transcript errors:** Manually correct misheard terms in Otter.ai.
- **AI hallucinations:** If ChatGPT invents facts, provide more context or break content into smaller chunks.

## Conclusion

This workflow reduces study guide creation time from hours to minutes, allowing you to focus on teaching. By leveraging Otter.ai, Notion, and ChatGPT, you can produce professional, accurate study materials effortlessly.
