## Overview

This workflow enables general practitioners to quickly cross-reference patient symptoms with the latest medical literature using AI tools, leading to more accurate diagnostic suggestions.

## Prerequisites

- A web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
- Free accounts for PubMed GPT and Perplexity AI
- A Notion account (free tier works)

## Step 1: Gather Patient Symptoms

- Use your EHR system or a simple note-taking app to list the patient's symptoms, duration, and relevant history.
- Example: "Chest pain, shortness of breath, fatigue for 2 weeks, history of hypertension."

## Step 2: Query PubMed GPT

- Open PubMed GPT (https://pubmed-gpt.com) in your browser.
- Enter the symptoms as a query: "Chest pain, shortness of breath, fatigue, hypertension"
- Review the AI-generated summary of relevant medical literature, including differential diagnoses and recent studies.
- Note key findings and potential diagnoses.

## Step 3: Validate with Perplexity AI

- Open Perplexity AI (https://www.perplexity.ai) in a new tab.
- Ask a focused question: "What are the common causes of chest pain with shortness of breath and fatigue in a patient with hypertension?"
- Perplexity will provide a concise answer with citations from recent medical sources.
- Compare the results with PubMed GPT to ensure consistency.

## Step 4: Organize Findings in Notion

- Create a new page in Notion titled "Diagnostic Suggestion - [Patient ID]"
- Use a template with sections: Symptoms, Literature Summary, Differential Diagnoses, Suggested Next Steps.
- Copy the key findings from PubMed GPT and Perplexity AI into the appropriate sections.
- Add your own clinical judgment and any additional notes.

## Step 5: Generate Diagnostic Suggestions

- Based on the cross-referenced literature, list the top 3-5 possible diagnoses.
- For each diagnosis, include supporting evidence from the literature and any conflicting information.
- Suggest further tests or referrals as needed.

## Step 6: Review and Document

- Review the diagnostic suggestions with a colleague or specialist if possible.
- Document the final differential diagnosis in the patient's EHR.
- Save the Notion page for future reference and follow-up.

## Tips for Efficiency

- Use browser extensions like Notion Web Clipper to save relevant articles directly.
- Create a shortcut to PubMed GPT and Perplexity AI on your browser's bookmark bar.
- Set up a recurring reminder to update your Notion template with new literature.

## Privacy Considerations

- Do not enter patient-identifiable information into AI tools. Use de-identified symptom lists.
- Ensure your browser and tools comply with HIPAA or local privacy regulations.
- Use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and consider a VPN for added security.
