## Overview

This workflow enables medical researchers to systematically cross-reference patient symptoms with clinical trials for rare diseases. By combining structured databases with AI-powered search, you can quickly identify relevant trials, extract key eligibility criteria, and compile evidence for case reports.

## Prerequisites

- A list of patient symptoms (structured or free-text)
- Basic familiarity with clinical trial databases
- Web browser with tab management (e.g., Chrome, Edge)

## Step 1: Collect and Structure Patient Symptoms

- Use a secure note-taking tool (e.g., Notion, Obsidian) to record symptoms in a structured format: symptom name, onset, severity, duration, and associated conditions.
- For rare diseases, include specific phenotypic details (e.g., "erythema nodosum" rather than just "rash").

## Step 2: Search ClinicalTrials.gov for Relevant Trials

- Go to [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov).
- Use the advanced search to combine symptoms and disease terms. For example: `("symptom" OR "phenotype") AND "rare disease"`.
- Filter by status: Recruiting, Not yet recruiting, Active.
- Export results as CSV or XML for further analysis.

## Step 3: Enrich with PubMed Literature

- Use [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov) to find case reports and studies linking symptoms to specific rare diseases.
- Search with MeSH terms: `(symptom) AND (rare disease) AND (clinical trial)`.
- Save relevant PMIDs for citation management.

## Step 4: AI-Powered Cross-Referencing with Perplexity AI

- Open [Perplexity AI](https://www.perplexity.ai) in a new tab.
- Use a prompt like: "List clinical trials for [rare disease] that include patients with [symptom]. Provide NCT IDs and eligibility criteria."
- Perplexity will search multiple sources and summarize findings. Verify with original sources.

## Step 5: Organize and Annotate with Zotero

- Install [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org) browser extension.
- Save trial pages, PubMed articles, and Perplexity results into a dedicated collection.
- Add tags for symptoms, disease, and trial phase.
- Use Zotero's annotation feature to highlight eligibility criteria related to symptoms.

## Step 6: Match Symptoms to Trial Eligibility

- Create a spreadsheet (Google Sheets or Excel) with columns: Symptom, Disease, Trial NCT ID, Eligibility Criteria, Match Score (High/Medium/Low).
- For each symptom, review the trial's inclusion/exclusion criteria. Mark as High if the symptom is explicitly listed, Medium if implied, Low if not mentioned.

## Step 7: Generate a Summary Report

- Use a template in Google Docs or Notion to compile findings.
- Include: patient symptom profile, matched trials with NCT IDs, rationale for match, and references.
- Share with the clinical team for patient enrollment consideration.

## Tips for Efficiency

- Use browser tab groups to organize trials by disease.
- Set up Zotero auto-sync to avoid losing references.
- For recurring searches, save Perplexity threads as templates.

## Privacy Considerations

- Never enter patient-identifiable information into public AI tools.
- Use de-identified symptom lists.
- Ensure compliance with HIPAA or GDPR when handling patient data.
