## Overview

This workflow enables corporate lawyers to quickly generate personalized contract clauses by analyzing a repository of prior agreements. Using AI tools like Claude, you can extract standard clauses, customize them for new contexts, and maintain a consistent legal style.

## Prerequisites

- A collection of prior agreements in digital format (PDF, DOCX, or TXT)
- Access to Claude AI (claude.ai) or similar LLM
- Notion account for clause library management
- (Optional) Zapier account for automation

## Step 1: Build a Clause Library in Notion

1. Create a new Notion database with properties: `Clause Name`, `Type` (e.g., Indemnification, Termination), `Source Agreement`, `Jurisdiction`, `Date Used`.
2. For each prior agreement, extract key clauses manually or using Claude (see Step 2) and add them as entries.
3. Tag clauses with relevant keywords (e.g., "SaaS", "M&A", "Governing Law").

## Step 2: Extract Clauses Using Claude

1. Open Claude and upload a prior agreement (PDF or DOCX).
2. Prompt: "Extract all standard clauses from this agreement. For each clause, provide the clause title, full text, and any key variables (e.g., party names, dates, amounts)."
3. Review Claude's output and copy the structured data into your Notion database.
4. Repeat for all prior agreements.

## Step 3: Generate a Personalized Clause

1. In Claude, describe the new contract scenario: "I need an indemnification clause for a software licensing agreement with a $1M cap, governing law of Delaware, and a 12-month survival period."
2. Ask Claude to search your Notion clause library (via integration) or manually provide relevant examples.
3. Prompt: "Based on the following prior clauses [paste 2-3 examples], generate a new clause that fits the scenario. Ensure language is consistent with our firm's style."
4. Review and edit the generated clause for accuracy.

## Step 4: Automate with Zapier (Optional)

1. Create a Zap that triggers when a new contract request is submitted via a form (e.g., Google Forms).
2. The Zap sends the request details to Claude via API to generate a clause.
3. The generated clause is saved to Notion and emailed to you for review.

## Best Practices

- Always review AI-generated clauses for legal accuracy and jurisdiction-specific nuances.
- Maintain a version history of clauses in Notion.
- Regularly update your clause library with new agreements.

## Troubleshooting

- If Claude misinterprets a clause, refine your prompt with more context.
- For large libraries, use Notion's search and filter to quickly find relevant clauses.
