## Step 1: Identify Core Keywords

Start by listing the primary keywords for your product page. Use **Google Search Console** to find queries that already drive traffic to your site. Focus on long-tail variations with question intent (e.g., "how to", "what is", "best way").

## Step 2: Mine Questions from Real Search Data

Use **AnswerThePublic** to expand your keyword list into actual questions people ask. Enter your core keyword and download the CSV. Filter for questions that are relevant to your product’s features or benefits.

## Step 3: Analyze Competitor FAQs

Visit top-ranking competitor product pages. Look for FAQ sections or “People also ask” boxes. Note the questions they cover and identify gaps you can fill. Use **Surfer SEO** to compare content structure and ensure your FAQ covers topics competitors miss.

## Step 4: Structure Your FAQ for SEO

Group questions by theme (e.g., pricing, features, setup). Use schema markup (FAQPage) to enable rich results. Write concise answers (40-60 words) that include your target keywords naturally. Use bullet points for readability.

## Step 5: Write and Optimize Answers

Draft answers that directly address the question. Include internal links to relevant product sections. Use **Surfer SEO** to check keyword density and readability. Aim for a score of 70+ on the Surfer content score.

## Step 6: Add Schema Markup

Implement FAQ schema using JSON-LD. Test with Google’s Rich Results Test. This increases chances of appearing in “People also ask” boxes.

## Step 7: Monitor Performance

Track FAQ clicks and impressions in Google Search Console. Update answers based on new search trends or product changes. Re-run AnswerThePublic quarterly to find fresh questions.
